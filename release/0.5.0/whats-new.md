Private, ad-free file sharing between nearby devices — no accounts, no cloud, no internet. This release adds new ways to share, on every platform.

## What's new

- **Send to several devices at once** — tick more devices in the send sheet and everyone gets the same files, with one progress screen and a Retry that resends only to the devices that failed.
- **In the share menu** — share photos, files, text or links from any app straight to CrossDrop (Android, iPhone/iPad/Mac). On Android, devices you've sent to before appear right in the share menu. On a Mac you can use **Open With** or drop files on the Dock icon; on Windows (installer build) right-click a file → **Send to → CrossDrop**. On every desktop you can drop files on the window.
- **Text and links** — send a note or a link; it arrives with **Copy** and **Open** instead of landing in the inbox.
- **Live camera** — CrossDrop's own camera on Android and iPhone: every photo or video you take is saved to your gallery and appears on the other device at once. The other device accepts once; the rest arrive without asking. Macs and PCs can watch.
- **Switch phones** — move the whole photo and video library and the contacts from one phone to another, Android ↔ iPhone in either direction. Photos keep their original dates, contacts already on the new phone are skipped, and a dropped connection picks up where it stopped. A Mac or PC can be the target too: it keeps a dated backup folder in Pictures.
- **Send a contact** — pick one or more contacts and send them; the other phone shows who it is and adds them with one tap (**Add to contacts**). Contacts shared from the Contacts app work the same way. Computers save the card as a `.vcf`.
- **Notifications** — Android now tells you about incoming transfers while CrossDrop is in the background.
- **Fixed on Windows:** transfer history and settings (like a renamed device) now keep every change instead of only the first.

Everything still travels straight between your devices over Wi-Fi, end-to-end encrypted. The new camera, photo and contacts permissions are only used by the features above — see the [privacy policy](https://github.com/GranTurismo/crossdrop/blob/main/PRIVACY.md). What testing found and what's still open is in [BUGS.md](https://github.com/GranTurismo/crossdrop/blob/main/BUGS.md).
