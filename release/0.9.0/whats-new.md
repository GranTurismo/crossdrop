Private, ad-free file sharing between nearby devices — no accounts, no cloud, no internet. This release connects two phones that share no Wi-Fi, and backs your phone up to your own computer.

## What's new

- **Connect anywhere** (Android) — can't see the other phone? Tap **Connect directly**: one phone shows a code, the other scans it inside CrossDrop, and the two see each other right away, with no Wi-Fi or internet involved. A phone without CrossDrop can still scan the same code with its camera and receive through "Send to anyone". Tapping a phone the radar only hears over Bluetooth now offers the same choice instead of doing nothing.
- **Back up to your computer** (Android → Mac, Windows, Linux) — Settings → **Back up to a computer**, pick your computer and check that both screens show the same code. From then on, whenever CrossDrop is open and both are on the same Wi-Fi, new photos and videos copy to `Pictures/CrossDrop Backup/<phone>/<year>/<month>` at full quality, with their dates and places. On the computer, Settings → **My devices** lists paired phones and where backups go.
- **Free up space** (Android 11+) — photos and videos older than 30 days that are safely on your computer can be deleted from the phone. The computer re-reads every file first, then Android asks you to confirm.
- **Photos keep their location** — Switch phones and backups now keep where each photo was taken; Android used to strip it from the copy.
- **iPhone and iPad** — no changes in this version; scanning a code arrives with the next App Store update.

Everything still travels straight between your devices, end-to-end encrypted. New on Android: the camera also reads codes, "media location" keeps photo places, and joining another phone's private link always asks you first — see the [privacy policy](https://github.com/GranTurismo/crossdrop/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).
