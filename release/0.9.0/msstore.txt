New: your phone can back up its photos and videos to this PC. Pair the phone once (both screens show the same code); from then on, whenever CrossDrop is open on both and they share a Wi-Fi, new photos and videos are copied to Pictures\CrossDrop Backup, one folder per month, with their original dates. Settings → My devices lists paired phones and where backups go, and can move the folder or unpair. Nothing goes over the internet.
