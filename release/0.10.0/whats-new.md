Private, ad-free file sharing between nearby devices — no accounts, no cloud, no internet. This release lets two people find each other by shaking their phones, and sends apps between Android phones.

## What's new

- **Shake to connect** (Android) — with several devices around, finding the right one takes a moment. Now two people shake their phones at the same time and each phone opens the other's send screen ("You shook phones with …"). It works over your Wi-Fi or a direct link, with CrossDrop open on the radar on both. The first time several devices are around, a short animation shows how. Settings → **Shake to connect** turns it off.
- **Share apps** (Android → Android) — tap a phone, then **Apps**: pick installed apps and send them, with all their parts. The other phone installs each one through Android's own installer, after allowing CrossDrop once; nothing installs by itself, and your data, accounts and purchases stay on your phone. Some paid apps and games also need Google Play on the other phone. The **Apps** tile only appears for Android phones.
- **Computers** — a phone that shakes, or links to your computer through a code, shows up on its radar right away. Devices now tell each other whether they are a phone or a computer, so CrossDrop only offers what the other side can use.
- **iPhone and iPad** — no changes in this version; scanning a code and shake to connect arrive with the next App Store update.

Everything still travels straight between your devices, end-to-end encrypted. New on Android: the motion sensor is read only while the radar is on screen (for shaking), and installing the apps you receive needs your permission once — see the [privacy policy](https://github.com/GranTurismo/crossdrop/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).
