Private, ad-free file sharing between nearby devices — no accounts, no cloud, no internet. This release brings My devices and the shared clipboard to iPhone, iPad and the Mac App Store version.

## What's new

- **My devices on iPhone and iPad** (version 1.6.0 on the App Store) — Settings → My devices → **Add a device**: both screens show a code, the other person agrees, and from then on the two send each other files without asking. A switch per device turns that off. Pairing works between any two devices that run CrossDrop: iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac, Windows, Linux.
- **Shared clipboard on iPhone and iPad** — turn on **Share clipboard** for a device, on both devices. iOS asks every time an app reads the clipboard, so CrossDrop never reads it by itself: when you come back to CrossDrop after copying something, the radar says "You copied something" and Apple's **Paste** button sends it with one tap. Copies from your other devices land on the clipboard while CrossDrop is open (iOS pauses apps in the background; a copy waits up to a minute).
- **Mac App Store version** — My devices and the shared clipboard too, and the clipboard works both ways by itself, like the Mac download.
- **Everything else** — the Android, Windows, Linux and Mac download apps have no changes in this release (they got the shared clipboard in 0.12.0); their version number moves with it. An iPhone that skipped 1.5.0 also gets its clearer failure messages and the rating prompt.

Everything still travels straight between your devices, end-to-end encrypted, and only between devices you paired. The translations of the new texts are machine-made: if one reads oddly, please tell us. See the [privacy policy](https://github.com/GranTurismo/crossdrop/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).
