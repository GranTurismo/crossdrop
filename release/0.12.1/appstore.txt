New: My devices. In Settings → My devices, add your own phones and computers: both screens show a code, you agree once, and from then on they send each other files without asking (a switch per device turns that off). Shared clipboard: turn on Share clipboard for a device, on both, then copy on one and paste on the other. On iPhone and iPad CrossDrop never reads your clipboard by itself: when you come back after copying something, tap Paste on the radar to send it, and copies from your other devices arrive while CrossDrop is open. On Mac it works both ways by itself. Also: clearer messages when a transfer fails, and after a few transfers that worked CrossDrop asks once for a rating. No cloud, no account, no ads.
