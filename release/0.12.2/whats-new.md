Private, ad-free file sharing between nearby devices — no accounts, no cloud, no internet. This release fixes two things on Android.

## What's new

- **A phone that shares its internet now shows up on the radar** — if you turn on your phone's hotspot and join a laptop or another phone to it, the two now see each other in CrossDrop and can send files right away. Before, they could only share through the QR code or the web page, because Android doesn't look for nearby devices on a network the phone itself hosts. CrossDrop now does that itself, on the hotspot only; on a normal Wi-Fi nothing changes. It works with the Mac, Windows, Linux and iPhone apps as they are: the update is only needed on the phone that hosts the hotspot.
- **Safer app installs** — when another Android phone sends you an app (Share apps), CrossDrop now checks that the install screen it opens belongs to Android's own installer before it opens it. This answers a security notice from Google Play; nothing looks different.
- **Everything else** — the Windows, Linux and Mac download apps and the iPhone/iPad app have no changes in this release; their version number moves with it (the iPhone and iPad app stays at 1.6.0).

Everything still travels straight between your devices, end-to-end encrypted. See the [privacy policy](https://github.com/GranTurismo/crossdrop/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).
