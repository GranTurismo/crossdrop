Private, ad-free file sharing between nearby devices — no accounts, no cloud, no internet. This release improves the page people see when you send them files without the app.

## What's new

- **Photos show as photos** — with "Send to anyone", the person who scans your QR code now sees your photos themselves on the page, not only their names. On an iPhone they can press and hold a photo to save it straight to Photos (the Save button still puts the file in the Files app). Other files are listed as before, and "Save all as .zip" still works.
- **A new look for the web page** — the page in their browser now matches the app: dark, with the mint accent.
- **Get CrossDrop from the page** — under the files, a button offers CrossDrop for the visitor's own device (Android, iPhone, Mac, Windows or Linux). The Wireless Drive page has the same link. The pages still load nothing from the internet, and following the link never passes your share's address to the store.
- **Microsoft Store** — the publisher name is now Gogita Kupatadze.
- **Everything else** — no other changes. Every app moves to 0.12.3 (the iPhone, iPad and Mac App Store app to 1.6.1).

Everything still travels straight between your devices, end-to-end encrypted. See the [privacy policy](https://github.com/GranTurismo/crossdrop/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).
