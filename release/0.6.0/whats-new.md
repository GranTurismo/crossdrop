Private, ad-free file sharing between nearby devices — no accounts, no cloud, no internet. This release is about making it easy for anyone to start.

## What's new

- **Nine new languages** — Georgian, Hindi, Indonesian, Arabic, Persian, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian and Turkish, on every platform. CrossDrop follows your phone's or computer's language list automatically, and a **globe button** on the radar, in the welcome tour and in Settings → Language lets you pick another one (or go back to "Automatic") instantly. Arabic and Persian read right to left. The translations are new: if something reads wrong, please [open an issue](https://github.com/GranTurismo/crossdrop/issues).
- **A welcome tour** — four short animated pages on first launch (find devices, send, send to anyone, private by design). Skip it any time; **Settings → Show the welcome tour** plays it again.
- **"Can't see the other device?"** — when the radar stays empty, or there is no network at all, CrossDrop now says so and offers a plain step-by-step guide (same Wi-Fi? guest or hotel network? hotspot?) instead of leaving you staring at a blank radar.
- **Connect directly (Android)** — no Wi-Fi around? One tap makes a private link from your phone and shows a QR code. The other person scans it with their camera, and the name and password are shown too for computers. Someone without the app can then use **Send to anyone**. It stops by itself after 30 minutes. On Android 12 and older, Android asks for Location before an app may do this; CrossDrop doesn't read or store where you are.
- **Polish** — dates and file sizes follow the chosen language, with the same digits in every language.

Everything still travels straight between your devices over Wi-Fi, end-to-end encrypted. Browser shares (**Send to anyone**) stay on your local network. The new hotspot feature uses the "change Wi-Fi state" permission and, on Android 12 and older, Location; both are used only for that feature — see the [privacy policy](https://github.com/GranTurismo/crossdrop/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).
