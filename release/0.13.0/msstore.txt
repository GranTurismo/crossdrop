Not on the same Wi-Fi as your phone? On the Android phone, tap Connect directly, then Make a CrossDrop Wi-Fi. On this PC, click Join a CrossDrop Wi-Fi: CrossDrop joins the phone's network for you, and forgets it again when you leave, so Windows never joins it by itself later. A PC without CrossDrop can join from the Wi-Fi list with the password shown on the phone. Everything stays end-to-end encrypted, straight between your devices.
