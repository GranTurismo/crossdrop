Not on the same Wi-Fi as the other phone? When it has made a CrossDrop Wi-Fi (Connect directly, then Make a CrossDrop Wi-Fi), tap Join a CrossDrop Wi-Fi on your iPhone or iPad and confirm: you're linked in one tap, no code to scan. On a Mac, pick the network in the Wi-Fi menu and type the short password shown on the phone. Everything stays end-to-end encrypted, straight between your devices.
