Private, ad-free file sharing between nearby devices — no accounts, no cloud, no internet. This release shares the clipboard between your own devices.

## What's new

- **Shared clipboard** (Android, Windows, macOS, Linux) — copy on one of your devices, paste on another. In Settings → My devices, turn on **Share clipboard** for a device, on both devices (both need this version). Text only.
  - **Computers** send each copy by themselves.
  - **Android phones** can only read the clipboard while CrossDrop is on screen, so a phone sends what you copied when you open CrossDrop (copies from the last 10 minutes), or straight away with the new **Share clipboard** tile in Quick Settings. Android shows its own "pasted from your clipboard" notice when that happens. Copies from your other devices land on the phone at once.
- **Private by design** — only devices you added in My devices take part, and each one has to prove the pairing first, so a device that copies a name gets nothing. Copies travel encrypted over your local network, never through the internet, and are never stored. Computers leave copies that password managers mark as secret alone.
- **iPhone and iPad** — no changes in this release. They can't be added to My devices yet, so the shared clipboard comes to them later.

Everything still travels straight between your devices, end-to-end encrypted. No new permissions. The translations of the new texts are machine-made: if one reads oddly, please tell us. See the [privacy policy](https://github.com/GranTurismo/crossdrop/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).
