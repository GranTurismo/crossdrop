Private, ad-free file sharing between nearby devices — no accounts, no cloud, no internet. This release makes sharing between your own devices effortless, sends whole folders, and finishes transfers that get cut off by itself.

## What's new

- **My devices** (Android, Windows, macOS, Linux) — Settings → My devices → **Add a device**: both screens show a code, the other person agrees, and from then on the two send each other files without asking. A switch per device turns that off. Files from anyone else still ask first, and a device that only copies a name never gets in.
- **Send when it's back** (Android, desktop) — pick files for one of your devices that isn't around and they go the next time it shows up, as long as CrossDrop is running on both. The radar lists your devices that are away; Settings → My devices shows what is waiting.
- **Finishes by itself** — if the connection drops mid-way (a Wi-Fi blip, a locked screen), the send waits for the device and carries on. What already arrived is kept, and the other side isn't asked again. If the person receiving cancels, they are asked before anything carries on.
- **Send a folder** (Android, desktop) — pick a folder and it arrives as the same folder with everything in it (up to 5,000 files).
- **Choose where files go** (Android, desktop) — Settings → Storage → Inbox folder. Only what CrossDrop received is ever listed or wiped there, never your own files.
- **Clearer problems** — a failed transfer says what happened and what to try ("Couldn't reach Anna's phone — open CrossDrop there and check you're on the same Wi-Fi"), and sending to an iPhone reminds you it only receives with the app open.
- **Rating** — after a few transfers that worked, CrossDrop asks once for a rating on Google Play or the App Store: never after a failure, at most three times, and nothing about how you use the app is sent.
- **Safer names** — file names in any script (Georgian, Hindi, Arabic…) keep their letters, and two different files can no longer overwrite each other. A device that sends you something can never put files into, or replace, your own folders and files in the inbox.
- **iPhone and iPad** — clearer failure messages, the rating prompt and the "keep it open" heads-up. My devices, folders and send-when-it's-back come to iPhone in a later update; for now an iPhone still asks before accepting, and shows a received folder's files without the folders.

Everything still travels straight between your devices, end-to-end encrypted. The translations of the new texts are machine-made: if one reads oddly, please tell us. The rating sheet is Google Play's or Apple's own; see the [privacy policy](https://github.com/GranTurismo/crossdrop/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).
